Learning Lab

How do emojis work?

An emoji can add information that words do not show directly. It can signal emotion, reaction, agreement, farewell, celebration or another social meaning. The exact interpretation still depends on the message and context.

😊

Friendly tone

Example: “Thank you 😊”
The emoji can make the message feel warmer.

👍

Agreement

Example: “Okay 👍”
It can signal approval or agreement.

👋

Farewell

Example: “See you 👋”
It can work as a farewell signal.

😮

Surprise

Example: “Wow! 😮”
It helps show a surprised reaction.

🎉

Celebration

Example: “We won! 🎉”
It adds a celebration signal.

😔

Sadness / regret

Example: “I’m sorry 😔”
It can strengthen a negative emotional tone.

Three questions to ask when you see an emoji

  1. What does the emoji usually communicate?
  2. What does it communicate in this exact message?
  3. Would the message feel different without it?
